Kitchen Scale

Connect your scale

  1. Turn the scale on.
  2. Tap its Bluetooth button until the icon starts blinking.
  3. Tap Connect and choose Vitamix Scale from the list.
This browser can't use Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. On iPhone or iPad, use the free Bluefy browser app. The page must be opened over https://.

The scale turns itself off after about 2 minutes without use.

    Scale setup

    Only needed for scales the app doesn't recognize, or if the weight doesn't match the scale's screen. The app learns the scale's format by comparing its data with the screen. Set the scale's screen to grams first.
